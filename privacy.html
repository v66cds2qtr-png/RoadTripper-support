Aussie Road Tripper Privacy Policy

Updated 3 October 2026.

Information on your device

Aussie Road Tripper saves trips, stops, wishlist places, home-base details, bookings, campsite reviews, attached documents and review photos, vehicle and maintenance records, expenses, checklists, travel-tool settings, and emergency information locally on your device. Trip photo references point to photos in your photo library.

The current app has no developer-operated backend, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, account registration or tracking feature. We do not receive your saved trip information through the app.

Your device's backup settings may include app data in backups managed by Apple. Aussie Road Tripper does not provide its own cloud-sync service.

Location and maps

With your permission, Aussie Road Tripper uses location to show your position, help select places and update emergency-location information. If you enable wishlist proximity alerts, it requests background location permission and uses significant location changes to check nearby saved places during active trips. Proximity alerts are local notifications. You can turn them off in Aussie Road Tripper Settings and change location and notification permissions in device Settings.

Maps, searches, geocoding, routes and map snapshots use Apple's services. Search terms and map or route coordinates may be processed by Apple to provide those features. Opening directions sends the selected destination to Apple Maps. Apple's handling of service data is governed by Apple's privacy policies.

Photos, documents and motion

Photo access lets you attach trip images and create postcards. Photo-saving permission lets you save exported postcards. Selected booking PDFs, park maps, personal caravan manuals and review photos are stored in the app. Imported caravan manuals are copied locally for offline access and included in full backups. Motion data powers the levelling tool; the app does not upload motion readings to a developer server.

Sharing and external links

Exports can contain personal information such as locations, bookings, photos, vehicle details and emergency notes. When you export a backup or use the share sheet, the destination you choose receives that information. Store exported files securely. External campsite websites and other services have their own privacy practices.

Your choices

Permissions are optional; some features need the corresponding permission. You can edit or delete saved entries using the app. Removing the app removes its local app data, but does not remove exported files, shared copies, saved postcards, photo-library originals or backups retained elsewhere.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact 15-weals.lotion@icloud.com. We will update this policy if Aussie Road Tripper's data practices change.

Support and this website

If you contact support, the information you choose to send is used to respond and investigate your request. Avoid sending private booking documents, full backups or emergency details unless requested. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages and contains no developer-added analytics scripts or tracking cookies. GitHub’s handling of website visits is covered by its privacy statement: GitHub’s privacy statement
