Help

The illustrated RoadTripper User Guide is included in the app under Settings → Help & Documentation and is available offline.

For a bug report, include the app version, device model, iOS/iPadOS version, steps to reproduce and any error message. Please avoid sending booking confirmations, full backups or emergency information unless needed and requested.

Back up your trips

Use Settings → Backup & Transfer → Export Full Backup before changing devices or reinstalling. Keep a separate secure copy. Importing a full backup replaces app data; review the app's confirmation before proceeding.

Permissions

Location, photos, motion and notifications can be managed in device Settings. Wishlist proximity alerts can also be disabled in RoadTripper Settings. Apple Maps services need network access for searches and road routes.

Your caravan manual

Open Tools → Caravan Manual → Import PDF and choose your own readable, unlocked PDF up to 20 MB. The app saves a local copy for offline use. Manual Options lets you replace, share or remove that copy. Removing it leaves the original file intact. Full backups include the saved manual.
